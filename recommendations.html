Recommendations

Practical suggestions to help reduce agricultural produce waste.

🌱 Recommendation Summary

Based on the current sample produce data, maintaining proper storage conditions and monitoring produce quality can help reduce potential post-harvest losses.

🏠
HIGH PRIORITY

Improve Storage

Proper storage can help protect agricultural produce from environmental conditions and quality deterioration.

  • Keep storage areas clean and dry.
  • Maintain suitable ventilation.
  • Check stored produce regularly.
🌡️
MEDIUM PRIORITY

Monitor Temperature

Temperature conditions should be monitored regularly, especially for produce that is sensitive to heat.

  • Record temperature regularly.
  • Avoid unnecessary heat exposure.
  • Take action when conditions change.
💧
MEDIUM PRIORITY

Monitor Humidity

Excessive moisture can contribute to quality problems in some types of stored produce.

  • Monitor humidity levels.
  • Prevent unnecessary moisture.
  • Keep storage areas ventilated.
🔍
LOW PRIORITY

Quality Monitoring

Regular quality checks can help identify deterioration early and support better decisions.

  • Inspect produce regularly.
  • Separate damaged produce.
  • Record quality changes.

📋 Suggested Action Plan

1

Check Storage

Inspect the current storage area and identify conditions that may affect produce quality.

2

Monitor Conditions

Keep track of temperature, humidity and produce condition at regular intervals.

3

Prioritize At-Risk Produce

Give earlier attention to produce showing signs of deterioration or higher waste risk.

4

Review the Analysis

Reassess the produce when its storage conditions or quality changes.

⚠️ Note: These recommendations are general prototype suggestions. Actual recommendations will later be generated according to crop type, storage conditions, environmental data and validated agricultural knowledge.
